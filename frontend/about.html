About Lost & Found Portal

Helping people reconnect with their lost belongings quickly, securely and efficiently.

Who We Are

The Lost & Found Portal is a web platform that helps users report lost and found items in one centralized system.

Instead of searching manually, users can upload item information, browse reports, and contact the rightful owner or finder.

The portal is designed to reduce the time required to recover lost belongings while making the reporting process simple and user-friendly.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a reliable platform where people can easily report lost and found items, making it easier to reconnect owners with their belongings.

Our Vision

We aim to become a trusted digital platform for schools, colleges, offices and public places to manage lost and found items efficiently.
